Public Guardrails

The teaching frame was reviewed and consolidated from a private source review, but the underlying messages, recordings outside the cleared player set, and evidence are intentionally withheld from the public version.

Do Not Diagnose

Use pattern language. Do not turn the model into a label, verdict, or accusation about a specific person.

Evidence Was Reviewed, Not Published

The public lesson is grounded in a private review and consolidation process. It does not publish messages, raw exports, ledgers, identifying dates, or song lyrics as proof.

Do Not Over-Blame

Choose songs and prompts that reveal difficulty, missed repair, capacity limits, and two-truth thinking without turning one person into the whole problem.

Teach the pattern, not the person.
