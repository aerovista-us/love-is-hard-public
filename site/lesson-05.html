Map the Escalation

Reactions matter, and so does the sequence before the reaction. The Borrowed War Loop gives the pattern a public-safe map.

Completion Suppression

A person cannot answer clearly if every sentence opens a new charge before it finishes.

Healthy version
One sentence, then response.
Distorted version
Interrupt, stack claims, then judge the reaction.
Try this instead
I will answer that after I finish this point.
Watch for
Watch for topic acceleration.
Reaction Capture

The reaction becomes the evidence while the build-up disappears.

Healthy version
We map before, during, and after.
Distorted version
Your frustration proves my whole story.
Try this instead
My reaction belongs to me; the sequence still matters.
Watch for
Watch for the last five seconds replacing the last five hours.
Borrowed War Loop

Pain may borrow an external battlefield: distress rises, gets assigned outward, becomes conflict, meets a boundary, escalates, crashes, then seeks repair.

Healthy version
Name the stage and interrupt the loop.
Distorted version
Treat one person as the war itself.
Try this instead
I care about the pain, and I am not joining the war.
Watch for
Watch for a repair request that restarts the same battle.
Practice: Map a composite conflict in seven stages: distress, assignment, pressure, boundary, escalation, crash, repair-seeking.
