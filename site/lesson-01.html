Lesson 1

Feeling Before Verdict

Name what is happening inside before assigning cause, motive, or blame.

Core Skill

Separate inner experience from the story attached to it.

Practice Line

"I feel alone. The story I am telling myself is that I do not matter. Can we check that together?"

Watch For

Words like always, never, obviously, and admit can turn feeling into verdict too fast.
