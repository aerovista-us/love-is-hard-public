Precision

Name the Pieces

Separate feeling, interpretation, claim, request, and boundary before a conversation turns into a verdict.

Feeling vs Claim

A feeling is inner data. A claim assigns cause, motive, or responsibility outside you.

Healthy version
I feel scared and need clarity.
Distorted version
You obviously do not care.
Try this instead
I feel ___, and the story I am telling myself is ___. Can we check it?
Watch for
Watch for always, never, obviously, and if you cared.
Interpretation Check

The first explanation may be compelling without being complete.

Healthy version
I am noticing a fear story and want to verify it.
Distorted version
My fear is proof that your motive is bad.
Try this instead
The meaning I made was ___. Is that what you meant?
Watch for
Watch for mind-reading and instant certainty.
Clean Request

A request names what would help without making the other person confess to a hidden charge.

Healthy version
Can we talk for 20 minutes at 6:30?
Distorted version
Prove I matter right now or this means everything.
Try this instead
What I am asking for is ___. Is that available?
Watch for
Watch for requests that require surrendering reality.
Practice: Rewrite one loaded sentence into five parts: what I felt, what I interpreted, what I claimed, what I needed, and what boundary or capacity limit was present.
