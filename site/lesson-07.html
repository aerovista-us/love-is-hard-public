Operations

Separate Love From Operations

Love, work, time, pace, public reputation, and role authority are different lanes.

Support vs Absorption

Care is not total emotional responsibility.

Healthy version
Brief support plus scheduled return.
Distorted version
You must fix my fear in real time or you do not love me.
Try this instead
I can support you for __ minutes, then I return at ___.
Watch for
Watch for regulation transfer.
Pace and Capacity

Urgency does not create availability.

Healthy version
This matters, and the workable window is 6:30.
Distorted version
If it matters, you must stop everything now.
Try this instead
I cannot do this while working. I can do ___ at ___.
Watch for
Watch for work focus being framed as rejection.
Public/Private Separation

A conflict does not become healthier when it moves into public retaliation.

Healthy version
Handle harm through direct repair, mediation, or safety channels.
Distorted version
Make the public pick a villain.
Try this instead
I will not litigate this publicly. I will address the behavior through the right lane.
Watch for
Watch for posts, proof displays, or reputation pressure.
Practice: Sort a conflict into lanes: love, operations, safety, public/private, and what each lane actually needs.
