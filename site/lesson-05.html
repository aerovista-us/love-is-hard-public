Lesson 5

Safety Gets Its Own Lane

Pattern language can describe conflict escalation. It cannot explain away fear, coercion, threats, or physical danger.

Stop Coaching

If safety is unclear, pause ordinary communication exercises and move to support, distance, or emergency help as appropriate.

Keep Separate

Do not fold safety claims into the proof-pressure loop. Safety needs its own review and its own plan.
