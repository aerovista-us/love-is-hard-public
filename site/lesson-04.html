Lesson 4

Boundary With Return

A boundary can protect repair when it names both the limit and the return.

Limit

"I cannot keep talking while we are escalating."

Return

"I can come back at 7:30 and listen for ten minutes."

Repair

"When we return, let's each name one thing we can do differently."
