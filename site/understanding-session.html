The 20-Minute Understanding Session

A guided conversation for one manageable issue, when both people feel safe participating. The purpose is to understand and repair—not prove who is right.

Start together · voluntary

Before you begin

Choose one low-stakes, recent situation. Each person should be free to speak, correct misunderstandings, request a pause, decline a request, or stop participating without punishment.

Safety comes before the exercise.

Do not use this tool to pressure someone into a confession, extract private information, demand forgiveness, or override a boundary. If there are threats, intimidation, coercive control, or fear of retaliation, stop the joint exercise and seek appropriate individual support. This is educational, not therapy or a crisis intervention.

Closing reflection

Understanding doesn't require agreement

Review what was heard and what both people voluntarily committed to. You can leave disagreement unresolved. Follow-through—not verbal victory—is the measure of repair.
