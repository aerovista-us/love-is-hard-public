Start Session 1: Name the Pieces

Begin the course

By the end, you should be able to:

  • separate a feeling from an interpretation and claim;
  • identify a hidden premise inside a request;
  • recognize when reassurance becomes proof pressure;
  • restore the full sequence before judging a reaction;
  • create a boundary that includes a responsible return;
  • separate love, work, safety, and public reputation into proper lanes;
  • build a shared protocol for recurring conflict.

Eight-session path

Name the pieces, hear clearly, expose hidden premises, recognize proof pressure, map escalation, protect boundaries, separate operations, and build an agreement.

01

Session 1: Name the Pieces

Feeling Before Verdict

Start with what you know: what you are feeling. Then separate the meaning you made from what has actually been observed or established.

10-15 minutesOutput: Five-Part SeparatorAudio lens: Head vs. Heart
02

Session 2: Hear Clearly

Hear Without Surrendering Reality

Hearing someone well does not require agreeing with every interpretation or accepting every premise.

10-15 minutesOutput: Shared Definition CheckAudio lens: After We Learned to Hear Each Other
03

Session 3: Hidden Premise

Find the Hidden Premise

A need can be valid while the demanded strategy is not. Hidden premises turn needs into accusations.

10-15 minutesOutput: Hidden-Premise RewriteAudio lens: Hearing Ain't the Same as Listenin
04

Session 4: Proof Pressure

Recognize Proof Pressure

Recognition becomes pressure when care must be proven again, immediately, and on a moving bar.

10-15 minutesOutput: Proof-to-Request ScriptAudio lens: Proof I Care
05

Session 5: Escalation Map

Map the Escalation

Reactions matter, and so does the sequence before the reaction. The Borrowed War Loop gives the pattern a public-safe map.

10-15 minutesOutput: Escalation Sequence MapAudio lens: How Does It Hurt Me
06

Session 6: Boundaries

Protect Boundaries and Repair Delivery

A boundary can hurt without being abandonment. Repair can include ownership without self-erasure.

10-15 minutesOutput: Boundary With ReturnAudio lens: Peace Ain't a Prison
07

Session 7: Operations

Separate Love From Operations

Love, work, time, pace, public reputation, and role authority are different lanes.

10-15 minutesOutput: Conflict Lane MapAudio lens: Nothing Left to Prove
08

Session 8: Agreement

Build an Operating Agreement

Convert recurring conflict into shared rules, review dates, and scripts before the next escalation.

10-15 minutesOutput: Operating AgreementAudio lens: Finding the Middle

Why this was built

Love Is Hard grew from a long-form review of how affection, misunderstanding, reassurance, boundaries, and repair changed over time. The private material remains private. What is published here is the repeatable pattern: the part that may help someone recognize a difficult cycle earlier.

Public Guardrails

Evidence was reviewed and consolidated, but private messages, raw recordings outside the cleared player set, and evidence are intentionally withheld from the public version.
