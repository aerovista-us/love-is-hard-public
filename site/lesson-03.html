Session 3: Hidden Premise

Hidden Premise

Find the Hidden Premise

A need can be valid while the demanded strategy is not. Hidden premises turn needs into accusations.

10-15 minutesOutput: Hidden-Premise RewriteAudio lens: Hearing Ain't the Same as Listenin
Need vs Strategy

The need may deserve care; the method demanded to meet it may still be negotiable.

Healthy version
I need predictable connection.
Distorted version
You must be available all day or I am not chosen.
Try this instead
The need is ___. A workable strategy could be ___.
Watch for
Watch for one strategy being treated as the only proof of love.
Hidden Claims

A request can quietly accuse: “do this” may mean “or admit you failed me.”

Healthy version
Can we schedule a check-in?
Distorted version
If you will not stop now, you do not value me.
Try this instead
The hidden premise might be ___. The clean ask is ___.
Watch for
Watch for compliments, apologies, and questions that carry charges.
Trust Paradox

An unfinishable test cannot build trust because every answer becomes the next test.

Healthy version
Ask once, receive, regulate, reconnect later.
Distorted version
Answer until the fear disappears completely.
Try this instead
I can answer once. If it does not land, we pause and return.
Watch for
Watch for retesting after reassurance was already given.
Practice: Take one demand and split it into need, strategy, hidden claim, and clean request.
