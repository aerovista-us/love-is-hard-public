Escalation

Recognize Proof Pressure

Recognition becomes pressure when care must be proven again, immediately, and on a moving bar.

Recognition vs Proof

Recognition says “I see this matters.” Proof pressure says “demonstrate it until my fear is gone.”

Healthy version
Thank you for naming it; I will let that land.
Distorted version
Say it again differently until I feel certain.
Try this instead
I can recognize the pain. I cannot enter an endless proof loop.
Watch for
Watch for admit, confess, prove, and if you loved me.
Moving Bar

The answer changes requirements after it is given.

Healthy version
Ask, answer, receive, regulate.
Distorted version
That answer does not count because now I need another form.
Try this instead
I answered the ask we named. If it still hurts, we need a pause.
Watch for
Watch for reassurance that is never allowed to land.
Confession Frame

Repair cannot require accepting an unverified premise as the entry fee.

Healthy version
I own interrupting and raising my voice.
Distorted version
I will only feel repaired if you admit you meant to hurt me.
Try this instead
I can own my behavior without confessing to a motive I do not believe is true.
Watch for
Watch for apology demands that erase boundaries.
Practice: Turn a proof demand into a time-bound request: one reassurance sentence, one return time, one self-regulation step.
